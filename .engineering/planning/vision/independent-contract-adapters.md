## Purpose

Record the operator's direction for independent adapters, shared semantic contracts, explicit authority and optional federation, with integration builders as the primary public audience.

## Source

[VISION.md](../../../VISION.md) owns the human-facing vision. Keep its prose there; this record owns its planning identity and relations. The existing [design handoff](../../../docs/design.md) supplies architectural decisions and historical evidence.

## Boundaries

The reviewed v0.1.0 specification milestone covers Kubernetes including discovery, GitLab and SQL. Existing first-slice runtime support and future semantic implementation are separate facts. Broader adapter and contract ambitions are direction, not scheduled or authorized runtime work.

## Acceptance

A reader can identify the intended users, practical use cases, architectural principles, non-goals, current milestone and observable long-term success criteria without reading the conversation or historical reviews.

## Authorization

The operator approved the human-documentation and website-design plan on 2026-09-09. This vision records that direction; it does not authorize publication, deployment or automatic implementation of its longer-term scope.
