## Outcome

The shared model has a typed home for a published tool contract: domain `connectors.tool_contracts`
with the profile's values, the per-operation declaration Connectors authors, and one entity, a
`ToolContract` an instance's descriptor revision publishes. Authored scenarios hold its one command to
the refusals the publisher makes, and the gate executes them against the pinned ESS interpreter.

## Design

- `ess/domains/tool_contracts.yaml`, provider-neutral (the ESS boundary gate holds it):
  - enums `Effect`, `Consequence`, `Risk`, `Idempotency`, `Determinism`, `Placement`, mirroring
    `tool-contract/1` § Profile (tools `contracts/profile/tool-contract-1.md` at tag 0.5.0);
    `human-visible`, `send-external`, `sensitive-data` and `non-idempotent` are spelled with `_`.
  - structs `Bound`, `Bounds`, `ToolProfile` and `OperationToolDeclaration`
    (`operation`, `name`, optional `profile`, `omit`).
  - entity `ToolContract` (identity `contract_ref`; `instance`, `descriptor_revision`, `toolset`,
    `version`, `operations`, `digest`), one state `Published`: a changed declaration or descriptor is
    a new contract.
  - command `PublishToolContract` whose `decision` is the publisher's, outcomes `published`,
    `profile-missing`, `schema-unrepresentable`, `risk-below-floor`, `name-invalid`; every refusal
    creates nothing and emits nothing.
  - ESS-LIMIT, recorded in the file: the risk floor over effects and consequences and the JSON Schema
    representability check are reductions ESS 0.69.2 cannot express, so `decision` is a trusted host
    value computed by `story:connector-projection`.
- `ess/components.yaml`: component `tool-contract-publisher` owns the domain.
- `contracts/tools/v1alpha1/semantics.md`: what a published contract guarantees and what it never
  carries (credentials, connection, grant).
- `contracts/tools/v1alpha1/scenarios/`, registered in `AUTHORED_DIRECTORIES`
  (`crates/connectors-build/src/gate.rs`).

## Scope

- `ess/domains/tool_contracts.yaml` (new), `ess/system.yaml`, `ess/components.yaml`
- `contracts/tools/v1alpha1/` (new)
- `crates/connectors-build/src/gate.rs` (`AUTHORED_DIRECTORIES` only)
- `website/docs/reference/contracts/` (regenerated by `connectors-docs generate`)

## Acceptance

- `ess specify validate --path ess` with ESS 0.69.2 answers valid.
- Authored scenarios, executed by the gate step `authored_scenarios`, all pass:
  - `tool-contract-published`: a publish with `decision: publish` creates one `ToolContract` in state
    `Published` and emits `ToolContractPublished`.
  - `tool-contract-refusals`: each of `profile_missing`, `schema_unrepresentable`, `risk_below_floor`
    and `name_invalid` answers its outcome and error, emits no `ToolContractPublished`, and the
    `ToolContracts` view holds no contract.
- `connectors-build ess-boundary` passes with the new domain.
