# Catalog

Optional discovery and distribution of adapter bundles.

The catalog design describes listing and locating precompiled adapter bundles with provenance and coverage. Directly configured adapter services do not require a catalog.

Catalog information grants no installation or execution authority.

The `provider` realization is implemented in the development checkout: one
executable, `connectors-catalog-provider`, loads a digest-verified bundle built
from a pinned OpenAPI document and exposes a declared selection of its operations
through the local CLI, with no Rust per endpoint. Reads are one bound GET; writes
are one bound POST, PUT, PATCH or DELETE under the host's approval, audit and
attempt controls, with an optional declarative preflight/postflight guard. The
GitLab bundle carries all 1,847 operations of the pinned source. A live GitLab has
answered merge-request reads, create and update through it. The index service
realization is not implemented.
