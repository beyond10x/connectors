# UXF-013: Exit statuses are stated mid-sentence in long paragraphs

The rotating card asks for one number: the exit status of `invoke --walk` without `--out`. The page
gives the walk's stop cases as a table, then the refusals as one nine-line paragraph where
"exit 2" is the twelfth word and the seven cases follow, separated by semicolons. The upgrade guide
does the same with "exit status 1" in a 14-line paragraph. Both tasks end on the highest reading
loads of the round.

One line: give the refusals the same table form as the stop cases (case, failure code, field,
exit status) in the contract source and the guide.
