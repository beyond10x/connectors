# UXF-007: Keyboard alone, the table of contents comes after the whole article

A keyboard user who wants a section of a long page tabs through every link of the article before
reaching the table of contents. On `/docs/reference/contracts/cli` that is several hundred presses.
A skip link to the table of contents, or the table of contents ahead of the article in DOM order,
belongs in Docs System's doc layout.
